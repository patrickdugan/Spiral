    Collateral
    Protected Cashu claims, each backed by a holder-exitable BTC reserve
    
            
    Mint
    Lock a claim in a trove and draw USD debt up to the collateral ratio
    
            
    Redeem
    Burn USD at face value for BTC from the lowest-ratio troves
    
            
    Collateral ratio
    Design target: a minimum ratio and a higher system-wide recovery ratio, as in Liquity. Values aren’t set.
    
            
    Liquidation
    Troves below the minimum ratio are absorbed by a stability pool. The reserve exit still protects the underlying holder.

    What the app can establish
    The demo uses synthetic payment evidence. Connected mainnet/testnet4 admission verifies M16 proof bytes and checks their active-chain context through Core. Isolated regtest admission checks real Core transactions and is labelled separately.
    A bound Cashu wallet can request issuance after admission. The proof desk stores blinded messages; the wallet keeps its secrets in an encrypted vault, unblinds the response, and verifies DLEQ signatures. A lost reply can be recovered using the original outputs. Read issuance state to see the provider stage.
    The encrypted wallet also supports regtest withdrawals into Ark, with reserved inputs and change recovery after a lost reply. A paid mint quote is one observation; the receiving Bark wallet must validate its VTXOs. Refresh successors and saved exit waves are checked against Core.
    A funded BitVM bridge, Filecoin deals and direct p2pool integration remain separate work. Payment inclusion does not establish reserve solvency, unspentness, VTXO validity or a successful exit. Local administrators still control the deployed code and database; Cashu remains mint-custodial.
