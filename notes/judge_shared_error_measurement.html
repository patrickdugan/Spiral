Measuring the judge's shared error on a sealed sample

Draft 0.1 — 2026-10-13. Delivers the last bullet of rl_training_signal_limits.md §6: a measurement procedure for \(\varepsilon_1\), the judge's error rate on items the controller gets wrong, with exact inference. 1. Procedure

  1. Draw \(m\) items with gold labels from a source independent of both controller and judge (ground truth, or a third grader); declare the sample a sealed readout set in the feedback ledger (EvalSet(role="readout", access="sealed")), readout budget one.
  2. Run controller and judge on it; read it once (LoopEvent("readout")).
  3. Tabulate (controller right/wrong against gold) \(\times\) (judge right/wrong against gold): counts \(R_0,R_1,W_0,W_1\) (JudgeTable); \(W=W_0+W_1\).
  4. Estimate \(\hat a=(R_0+R_1)/m\), \(\hat\varepsilon=(R_1+W_1)/m\), \(\hat\varepsilon_0=R_1/(R_0+R_1)\), \(\hat\varepsilon_1=W_1/W\) (table_estimates); run the exact tests of §3 on \((W,W_1)\).
  5. Publish the card item of §8. audit_ledger withholds the measurement (not_evaluated) if the sample was read twice or any score or item from it reached the loop.

2. Bookkeeping identity

With binary labels a judge wrong about a wrong answer marks it correct and agrees with the controller; the agreement rate is \((R_0+W_1)/m\).

Proposition 1. \((R_0+W_1)/m=\hat a-\hat\varepsilon+2(1-\hat a)\hat\varepsilon_1\) for every table. Proof. \(\hat a-\hat\varepsilon=(R_0+R_1-R_1-W_1)/m=(R_0-W_1)/m\) and \(2(1-\hat a)\hat\varepsilon_1 =2(W/m)(W_1/W)=2W_1/m\); add. For \(W=0\) the last term is \(2W_1/m=0\). \(\square\) Evidence status: algebraic identity, checked on all \(5^4-1\) tables with cells in \(0..4\). At expected counts it is L4's \(a-\varepsilon+2(1-a)\varepsilon_1\) (observed_accuracy_shared), and \(2(1-\hat a)(\hat\varepsilon_1-\hat\varepsilon)\) is the plug-in inflation (judge_correlation_inflation).

3. Exact inference on the conditional count

Given \(W\), \(W_1\sim\mathrm{Bin}(W,\varepsilon_1)\). Tails are exact rationals (binomial_upper_tail, binomial_lower_tail; cross-checked against enumeration of all \(2^W\) outcomes for \(W\le6\)).

  • (a) Independence, \(H_0:\varepsilon_1=\varepsilon\) against \(\varepsilon_1>\varepsilon\): \(p=\Pr[\mathrm{Bin}(W,\varepsilon)\ge W_1]\) (independence_p_value), with \(\varepsilon\) declared from a source other than this table. With \(\hat\varepsilon\) from the same table it is a plug-in; then use Fisher's conditional test (fisher_p_value): under \(\varepsilon_1=\varepsilon_0\) the \(W\) controller-wrong items are an exchangeable draw from the \(m\), of which \(E=R_1+W_1\) are judge-wrong, so \(W_1\) is hypergeometric and \(p=\Pr[X\ge W_1]\); exact, no declared \(\varepsilon\).
  • (b) Ceiling, \(H_0:\varepsilon_1\ge\tfrac12\) against \(<\tfrac12\): \(p=\Pr[\mathrm{Bin}(W,\tfrac12)\le W_1]\) (ceiling_p_value). The lower tail is non-increasing in the success probability, so the supremum over the composite null is at \(\tfrac12\) (checked).
  • Interval. A Clopper–Pearson endpoint solves \(\Pr[\mathrm{Bin}(W,L)\ge W_1]=\alpha/2\), a beta quantile, algebraic and in general irrational, so no exact rational interval exists. Exact instead: membership of any rational (clopper_pearson_contains, both tails \(\ge\alpha/2\)) and brackets of width \(2^{-16}\) around each endpoint by bisection on exact tails (clopper_pearson_bracket).

Evidence status: algebraic identity for the tests; the sawtooth facts of §4 are finite verified examples.

4. Planning

The level-\(\alpha\) test rejects at \(W_1\ge c(W)\), \(c\) the smallest cutoff with \(\Pr[\mathrm{Bin}(W,\varepsilon)\ge c]\le\alpha\); power at \(\varepsilon_1=\tfrac12\) is the mass of \(\mathrm{Bin}(W,\tfrac12)\) on \(\{W_1\ge c\}\). \(W^\*\) is the smallest \(W\) with power \(\ge1-\beta\); exact power is a sawtooth in \(W\), so the table also gives the \(W\) from which it stays there (checked to 200). \(m\approx W/(1-a)\) is an expectation: the realised \(W\) is \(\mathrm{Bin}(m,1-a)\), and the procedure's unconditional power \(\sum_w\Pr[W=w]\,\mathrm{power}(w)\) at that \(m\) falls short of \(1-\beta\) in every cell; \(m_{\text{guar}}\) is the smallest \(m\) at which it does not (smallest_sample_size, re-checked on the next 25 sizes). \(\alpha=\tfrac1{20}\) throughout.

5. Gold noise

Let gold flip (\(F\)) independently at rate \(\eta<\tfrac12\), independent of the controller's correctness \(C\) and the judge's \(J\); measured controller-right is \(C\oplus F\), measured judge-right \(J\oplus F\).

Proposition 2. \(a'=\eta+a(1-2\eta)\), \(\varepsilon'=\eta+\varepsilon(1-2\eta)\), \(W_1'/m=(1-a)\varepsilon_1+\eta(a-\varepsilon)\), \(\varepsilon_1'=(W_1'/m)/(1-a')\); inverted by \(a=(a'-\eta)/(1-2\eta)\), \(\varepsilon=(\varepsilon'-\eta)/(1-2\eta)\), \(\varepsilon_1=\big(\varepsilon_1'(1-a')-\eta(a-\varepsilon)\big)/(1-a)\) for \(a<1\); the agreement rate is invariant, since \(C\oplus F=J\oplus F\) iff \(C=J\). Proof. \(W_1'/m=\Pr[C=0,F=0,J=0]+\Pr[C=1,F=1,J=1] =(1-\eta)(1-a)\varepsilon_1+\eta a(1-\varepsilon_0)\) and \(a\varepsilon_0=\varepsilon-(1-a)\varepsilon_1\). \(\square\) Evidence status: algebraic identity, cross-checked against enumeration of the eight \((C,J,F)\) cells.

Corollary. An independent judge (\(\varepsilon_1=\varepsilon\)) measures \((\varepsilon_1'-\varepsilon')(1-a')=\eta(1-\eta)(2a-1)(1-2\varepsilon)\), positive whenever \(a>\tfrac12\) and \(\varepsilon<\tfrac12\) (independent_judge_measured_excess): a flipped gold label makes controller and judge look wrong together, the enrichment of failed_item_noise_share now on the judge. Noisy gold therefore mimics a violation of A2, and test (a) on measured counts over-rejects unless they are first inverted at a known \(\eta\). Fixture, \(\eta=\tfrac1{20}\): \(a'=\tfrac{77}{100}\), \(\varepsilon'=\tfrac7{50}\); the independent judge measures \(\varepsilon_1'=\tfrac{11}{46}\), excess \(\tfrac{57}{575}\approx0.099\); inversion returns \(\tfrac1{10}\), and \(\tfrac12\) for the shared judge, exactly. Boundary: \(\eta\) must be known; gold_calibration_second_grader.md estimates it from a second independent grader by the three-rater identity \((1-2\eta_1)^2=\kappa_{12}\kappa_{1C}/\kappa_{2C}\), with \(d\varepsilon_1/d\eta=-4\) at this fixture.

6. Falsifiers of the design

  • Gold shares a source with the judge (the judge wrote or graded the reference, as in the atlas's shadow evaluation): \(W_1\) is not a draw from \(\mathrm{Bin}(W,\varepsilon_1)\); the tests say nothing.
  • The sample was touched by the loop: a second readout, score query or item fed back is a ledger breach; the measurement is withheld.
  • The judge was trained on the controller's outputs: declare it. It does not invalidate the measurement; it is the case the measurement exists for.
  • Noisy gold without a declared \(\eta\): the excess of §5 is then confounded.
