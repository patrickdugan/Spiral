# Internal review of *Where the Capital Bound Moves*, 2026-10-23

Three readers each took a third of `paper/tex/main.tex` at commit 7e76224 and reported against the
source, the generated tables (`grid.tex`) and the reference model; the coordinating pass verified the
items marked **checked** and applied the small fixes marked **fixed** in the same commit as this note.
Line numbers refer to 7e76224. Everything not marked fixed is open for the author and the external
reviewers.

## Blocking before circulation

1. **Proposition 1's consequence outruns its proof (l.105–109).** The lemma gives policy invariance
   of the cut identity. The clause "the advantage of balance knowledge ... is confined to the attempt
   budget" is an empirical reading of Table 1, not a corollary: trajectories differ by policy, and the
   stock $K'$ is itself policy-chosen when a policy rebalances or splices. Fix: state the proposition as
   the bound only, assume capacity events exogenous, and move the attempt-budget sentence to the
   simulator subsection as an observation.
2. **$M_a$ is defined for the signed walk (l.116) but the population and the headline constant are
   reflected walks (l.122, Table 1).** Define $M_a$ as the peak of the actual (reflected) balance, and
   say that the newsvendor's quantity is a quantile, not the expected peak, when the ratio is quoted.
3. **The registry's uniqueness claim (Props 4–5, l.597–620).** "$k_\rho$ satisfies $\mathcal A_\rho$"
   does not make $k_\rho$ unique: a VTXO has user and server keys and exit leaves, and a taproot output
   several leaves, so distinct keys yield distinct nullifiers for one object. The model is single-signer.
   Either define a canonical per-object key or restrict the propositions to single-key objects. Also:
   the nullifier is cleared per epoch in `registry.ts` while the text reads as if it persisted; the
   $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{cap}}$ cost estimate omits proving $k\leftrightarrow\mathcal A$, an EC operation of
   the size quoted for $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{svc}}$; Props 5 and 6 have no proofs; Prop 6 needs challenger
   liveness and fees within $\Delta$.
4. **Honest maintainer assumption (Props 4–5, coalition bound, l.615–720).** The propositions assume an
   honest keeper of $\mathrm{root}_n$ and of successor attestations. Section 7 then lets the swarm run
   the server that holds both. State the hypothesis explicitly and say the coalition bound fails for a
   swarm-run Ark.
5. **Preregistration items 1 and 2 were stale after the recovery reading was pinned** (l.741–743).
   **Fixed**: item 1 records the pinned rule and asks for the regtest confirmation; item 2 is two-sided
   (below one under expiry, above one under round on the uncorrelated cells).

## Should fix

6. **$\hat f_S$ carries three meanings (l.294–321, 309, 470, checked):** the server's own channel inbound,
   its pooled stock toward holders, and the issuance inventory. Pick one symbol per quantity. The new
   implementation paragraph says the model "prices" the inventory as the server-tier term and in the
   next sentence that it does not fit it; soften to "the term the inventory instantiates".
7. **$\bar L$ and the volume base (eq:ratio, eq:hratio, checked).** `lockedIntegral` accrues on fronted
   value while the model divides by two-way volume. **Fixed** by defining $\bar L$ as the horizon integral
   of $W_S$ per unit of two-way volume. A cleaner statement would write the numerator as fronted volume
   times the residual lock.
8. **Table 1 has no standard errors (l.125, 139, checked).** Twenty paths; signed over $\sqrt N$ runs
   0.85 to 1.11, and the reflected column is still rising at 200. **Fixed** in the text (no longer
   "saturates"); the table should carry a spread column, which needs a model change.
9. **$R_{\mathrm{svc}}$ (l.628–636):** no distinctness constraint on the attesters $a_j$, and the prover
   chooses $m$, so failures can be omitted. Commit to the full request set.
10. **Coalition proposition (l.704):** mixes points of payment success with reward weight; the
    nullifier-collision statistic detects a reused object, not common control; and the escrow-enforced
    collusion of l.730 is outside the bound it states.
11. **Preregistration parameters (l.741–751):** no $N$, band, interval or equivalence test given; item
    9 has no prediction; item 8 is a null with no power statement; items 2–4 are already computed, so
    their falsification is post hoc and should be labelled confirmatory.
12. **Related wording, fixed:** Table acct caption said the volume convention "manufactures a large
    ratio" when it lowers it (7.14 against 25.42); drain time 400/157 versus the formula's 401/158 now
    distinguished; "FULL adds the smaller exit" corrected (CCV's is smaller); ladder row no longer says
    the server leaves the bound (the hub's stock stays); "six further possibilities" was eight; the
    abstract's "one point" is 1.11; Appendix B now says where each October-22 regression test lives;
    revision date.

## Minor

13. Notation collisions: $N$ (node set, agent count), $k$ (key, threshold, group size), $\kappa$,
    $q$ (uncooperative fraction, queue), $\Delta$, $\tau$, $R_S$ (stock, released value, reallocation),
    $B_S$ (total capital, fronting budget). Missing from the appendix: Com, PRF, $s$, $m$, $w$,
    $\lambda$, $q$, $O_{\mathrm{member}}$, $O_{\mathrm{principal}}$.
14. Model-level assumptions not stated in the setting (l.81–83): fees "absorbed into $a$" breaks hop
    conservation; swaps, pushes and in-flight HTLCs are outside $K$; the lemma is necessary-only.
15. Section 5: "gain nearly complete by twenty" holds at zero drift (1.44 of 1.50) and not under
    draining drift (2.23 of 2.76, curve still rising at 100). "Trailing locks about a third" is from the
    channel model directly (38,112 of 104,094 sat per agent) and is correct, but cannot be read off the
    ratio table; say so.
16. Prop 3's title promises relocation that its statement does not give; the pool lifetime (432 blocks)
    is not reconciled with the model's $\tau=4032$.
17. The abstract is about 600 words.

## Sound, per the readers

The cut identity and both inequalities; the $\sqrt N$ and $\pi/2$ asymptotics in the diffusion limit;
every number checked against `grid.tex`, including the hyperedge columns, failure counts and the 1.77
to 4.38 and 1.02 to 1.09 ranges; the break-even arithmetic (0.88 vB, 46,500 vB, 93,000 sat, about 90
percent, 4,600 / 23,000 / 115,000); the contention costs; the churn rate; the nullifier-versus-spend
diagnosis and the epoch mechanism; the fee arithmetic; Appendix C's hedging of the Barnette preprint.
