# Where the capital bound moves in federated derivatives markets

Research extension for *Where the Capital Bound Moves: Settlement Objects, Proof Economics, and Agent Coordination on Bitcoin Payment Networks*. Manuscript base: Spiral main at 29082373817c4065e3237cd37f5e1463b0822a10, paper/tex/main.tex, Sections 3–6 (especially Proposition bound relocation, liquidity-duration definition, and proof economics). Parallel to the Cashu reserve and Testnet4 campaigns. Candidate section, not an accepted or rebuilt manuscript.

## 1. Market access and the resource that can be priced

SHZ HFT-04 proposes a 200 ms early oracle stream and a fee split. The central exchange can price priority at its own queue because liquidity behind that queue is not directly accessible elsewhere. An oracle relay that republishes a substitutable upstream signal has no comparable exclusive matching right.

Conditional contestability result: fix a signed order, identical execution opportunities (including depth, queue rights, latency, safety and reliability), identical collateral/settlement access, negligible switching cost, at least two independent providers with the same constant marginal service cost c and no binding capacity or entry constraint. In the simple price-only Bertrand game with divisible prices and buyers choosing the lower price, any common price p>c admits a profitable undercut; p=c is an equilibrium. This is a conditional no-positive-markup result, not a claim that actual routers are identical or free. The argument does not apply to distinct liquidity, exclusive order flow, licensing, capacity scarcity, actual settlement attestations, differentiated service or fixed costs. Oracle price discovery and the authority to attest the specified DLC event are distinct services. Proof that someone published a price first does not convey exclusive access to the underlying market. HFT-04's 70/30 revenue split remains a test tariff, not a durable revenue forecast.

The candidate scarce resource is enforceable, correctly placed BTC collateral over time, plus exit/settlement capacity. LPs may earn competitive capital carry or lease fees for supplying it. Clearing providers may charge for demonstrated cost reductions or for operational service. Any economic rent above opportunity cost depends on scarcity, differentiation, bargaining and competition. Decentralization does not itself eliminate market-access or clearing-network effects, or legal obligations.

## 2. Comparable settlement histories

Fix a horizon H, order/fill and obligation history, maturity schedule, shock/default path, and equivalent enforceable customer claims. Compare isolated bilateral objects (baseline 0) against independent routers with multilateral clearing (candidate 1). Hold matched notional V constant, once per trade, in a declared BTC numeraire; if quoted in USD, apply the same execution-time BTC conversion in both arms. Also report the paper's original liquidity duration using the same gross delivered claim/payment volume in both arms, and report gross versus net physical payment flow separately. Never let the candidate denominator shrink merely because payments net. For unequal delivered service compare a matched subset and report rejected/unserved demand; don't use the same equation to compare different service levels silently.

Inventory each distinct satoshi backing an enforceable object exactly once at each time t. A category partition includes resting/committed trader margins, paid-but-not-recovered fronting, dedicated liquidity/idle capacity, pending settlement obligations backed by reserved funds, provider bonds, exit/fallback funding and other incremental reserves. A bookkeeping pending obligation that is already backed by counted collateral is not another capital object. Fees are transfers, not double-counted system resource cost. Owner tags allow trader vs provider incidence.

Let A_j = integral_0^H K_j(t) dt in BTC-years, with K_j the total distinct locked BTC; A_j/V has units years, a matched-notional-normalized capital duration. Under common simple annual BTC funding rate r, capital component of unit cost in basis points is 10^4 r A_j / V. The gross incremental capital saving is 10^4 r (A_0-A_1)/V. For heterogeneous funding rates use sum_i integral r_i(t) K_ij(t) dt, with time in years. Price Bitcoin blockspace, compute, data, recovery, proof and operational costs separately in BTC at consistent conversion times. Include incremental risk cost (or constrain default, exit, liveness and loss distributions explicitly). Annual simple carry is a scenario assumption; use actual borrowing/lease rates, term structure and discounting where available.

## 3. Fee feasibility proposition (accounting identity)

For the same service let C0U be all baseline customer costs, C1U the candidate customer costs before the newly proposed clearing fee, and C1P incremental provider costs for the service (including its own capital carry, proofs, liquidity maintenance and required risk provision, net of any externally funded revenue). All costs use the same horizon and numeraire. Any expense booked to one party is not also booked to the other. Let F be the total fee paid by users to the provider.

Aggregate customer participation requires F <= C0U-C1U. Nonnegative provider economic profit requires F >= C1P. Thus a fee feasible for both exists iff C0U-C1U >= C1P, and incremental economic profit satisfies

    Pi = F-C1P <= C0U-C1U-C1P.

Proof: rearrange C1U+F <= C0U and F-C1P >= 0. This is a weak aggregate condition; individual rationality requires each user's own savings to cover its allocation of F (or agreed side payments), and outside options may be better than the chosen baseline. If a baseline provider must also be paid or costs already include its fees, specify the commercial counterfactual; compare real resource costs for system efficiency and customer all-in costs for pricing. The user's maximum acceptable fee is not automatically the provider's profit. Under the simplifying common-rate, cost-only, equivalent-service comparison, net system surplus per traded BTC in bps is 10^4 [r(A0-A1)+O0-O1]/V, where O are other real resource costs. Negative surplus rules out a cost-saving justification under these assumptions.

This puts a bound on value created by clearing and an outside-option bound on monetization. It is not a theorem of market share or realized revenue.

## 4. Settlement flow is not the margin requirement

For matured, simultaneously enforceable obligations g_ij >= 0, write b_i = sum_j g_ji - sum_j g_ij and G = sum_ij g_ij. In an unrestricted frictionless clearing graph with no fees, sufficient transferable settlement inventory and admissible atomic discharge, the smallest amount of direct debtor-to-creditor transfers is N = sum_i max(b_i,0) = (1/2) sum_i |b_i| <= G. Every net creditor must receive its positive net balance; greedily match net debtors and creditors to attain the bound. The identity uses matured obligations and does not reduce the collateral backing unexpired independent DLCs. For A->B=5 and B->A=5, G=10 and N=0, although both contracts may still require segregated margin before maturity. Margin release is a separately authenticated state transition, not a consequence of N=0.

With vault budgets C_v and eligible payout edges of capacity u_vj, the restricted payout graph has full delivery iff for every creditor subset J:

    sum_{j in J} d_j <= sum_v min(C_v, sum_{j in J} u_vj).

This is the max-flow/min-cut condition for source-to-vault-to-creditor-to-sink network with demands d_j. Derive it by minimizing a cut for fixed sink-side creditor set J; each vault independently contributes either its source capacity or its edges into J. Record the deficit when the inequality fails. Multi-hop, time, commitment and script constraints require the actual expanded graph. A prover should verify a feasible supplied settlement and exact reserve changes, not claim that proving a feasible flow creates capacity. A min-cost flow relaxation with capacitated edges can report dual shadow prices of additional directional liquidity under a fixed demand/cost model; these are marginal planning values, not observed market prices or guaranteed earnings.

Legal/contractual netting eligibility, matched claim identity, risk under correlated BTC moves, price rounding/clipping, timeouts, finality, unilateral exits, offline signers, proof availability and reorg handling constrain release. A clearing arrangement that adds a senior claim, weakens exit rights, needs a new custodian, or shifts an equal lock to an LP has changed the resource or security comparison. Price all new reserves, including proof/dispute bonds and bridge/LP capital that remains locked while traders get available credit. Never treat a 100 ms fill batch as 100 ms final collateral recovery. Current SHZ reference router keeps committed margins encumbered; its journal/ack gate does not yet authenticate real LDK persistence or payments.

## 5. Scale calibration

Illustrative only: two 10x parties require 0.2 BTC of combined initial margin per BTC of matched notional at entry; suppose 93.75% of that margin (0.1875 BTC per BTC traded) can be made reusable earlier without replacement capital or weaker rights. This fraction is a scenario, not a claim about SHZ's average capital requirement or independently certified future release. At r=5% annual BTC carry, saving an interval Delta days gives f_bps = 10^4*0.05*0.1875*Delta/365. See shz_clearing_rent_scenarios.json for calculated examples.

A 2 bp fee needs about 7.79 days of earlier recovery at those assumptions before other expenses. A one-day saving is about 0.257 bp; an hour is 0.0107 bp. A 100 ms saving is economically negligible under pure carry. Trading frequency alone therefore cannot justify a 2 bp tax on every fill. Avoid counting one physical collateral release repeatedly across all fills that reused the same stock; the authoritative measure is the time integral of unique locked stock.

If trader capital is released but LP/bond capital is locked instead, recompute the total and its ownership incidence. A provider can sell a valuable service even if aggregate physical capital is unchanged, through differences in funding rates, operational costs or risk tolerance; that benefit must be measured rather than assumed.
