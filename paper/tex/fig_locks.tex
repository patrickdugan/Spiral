\caption{Locked capital-time, the quantity the liquidity duration (Definition~2) divides by delivered volume, drawn as bars whose area is capital times time. A channel's stock toward an agent is held for the horizon whether or not the agent's peak arrives. Under either reading the server's pooled stock $\hat f_S$ is held likewise, forecast once for all holders. What differs between the readings is the forfeit lock $W_S$: value the server fronts at a spend is reusable one round later under the round reading, or only when the spent output would have expired under the expiry reading, so the mean residual lock $\bar L$ is a round in one case and about half a lifetime in the other; the third bar runs past the horizon. The implementer's documentation does not settle which reading holds; Table~\ref{tab:grid} carries both, and the first preregistered item is to pin it.}
