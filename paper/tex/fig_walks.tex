\caption{Four agents of the zero-drift cell, drawn with the model's generator over \walkT{} rounds. Each balance is a random walk reflected at zero, since an agent spends only what it has received. Per-agent provisioning must hold each agent's own peak, and the sum of those peaks is the dashed line; a pool must hold the peak of the sum, the dotted line. The gap between the two lines is the whole pooling gain. It is a constant factor and not $\sqrt N$, because the reflected walks all sit above zero and never cancel one another (Remark~\ref{rem:sqrtN}). Generated by \texttt{model/grid.ts}.}
