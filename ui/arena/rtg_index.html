Reading the scene

Figures are scripted trait vectors placed at their venue. A figure's base and halo carry its state; an amber halo and a nervous sway are graded stress before a flip. Strike beams are coloured by the exploited vector; the dark octahedron is the adversary's presence, hovering over its last target. The annals narrate exported frame events as abstract status lines only.

Hive vision (default) is the adversary's perspective: devices burn bright as entry points and carry their vector tags, cash is priced (funds, values, the price to turn a custodian, data stores), the hottest marks glow magenta by their exploitable surface and the hive's gaze lines reach for the three hottest. What the hive cannot know stays hidden there: a decoy reads as a data store until it burns, and no truth class is shown. Switch it off for the researcher's view.
