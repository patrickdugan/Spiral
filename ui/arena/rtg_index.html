Reading the scene

Figures are scripted trait vectors placed at their venue. A figure's base and halo carry its state; an amber halo and a nervous sway are graded stress before a flip. Strike beams are coloured by the exploited vector; the dark octahedron is the adversary's presence, hovering over its last target. The annals narrate exported frame events as abstract status lines only.

Infrared (H) is the hive's overlay on the city: the scene goes cold and only the juicy bits show hot. Marks glow on a thermal scale by their exploitable surface, devices burn white-hot as entry points and carry their vector tags, cash is priced (funds, values, the price to turn a custodian, data stores), the hive's gaze lines reach for the three hottest marks, turned units cool to embers and units the hive has not found stay dark. What the hive cannot know stays hidden there: a decoy reads as a data store until it burns, and no truth class is shown. The default view is the city itself, coloured by the sim's state.
